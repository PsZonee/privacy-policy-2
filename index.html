Knife Slash — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026 · Developer: MASARP STUDIO ("we", "us") · Contact: sammanlamsal9@gmail.com

Knife Slash is a football penalty-shootout game published as a Facebook Instant Game. This policy explains what information the game does and does not handle when you play it.

1. Information we do NOT collect

The game is built to run with zero permissions. We do not ask for, access, or store:

  • Your name, profile photo, or Facebook ID
  • Your friend list or contacts
  • Your email address or phone number
  • Your location
  • Any payment information (the game has no purchases)

2. Game progress data

To make the game work, your progress is saved: coins, match results (wins, draws, losses), your best score, power-up inventory, and ladder standing. Inside Facebook, this data is stored using Facebook's own player-data storage for this game; outside Facebook it is stored locally on your device. This data is used only to run the game — restoring your progress, showing your best score, and setting the target in challenges you send. We do not use it for advertising profiles, and we never sell it.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads served by Meta Audience Network (banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads). Meta may collect and use device and advertising information — such as device identifiers, ad interactions, and usage signals — to serve and measure ads, as described in Meta's Privacy Policy and Meta's advertising policies. We do not control what Meta collects through its ads and we do not receive personal data from it; we receive only aggregated ad-performance and revenue reporting.

4. Challenge sharing

If you tap "Challenge a Friend" or "Send Challenge", the game uses Facebook's share dialog to send a challenge card that you created. The shared content contains only game information: your score and the positions of your shots. Sharing happens only when you choose to send it, through Facebook's interface, and is governed by your Facebook settings.

5. How information is used

  • To save and restore your game progress
  • To run matches, ladders, and friend challenges
  • To show ads that keep the game free
  • To understand, in aggregate, whether the game is working correctly

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, other than Meta/Facebook as described above (player-data storage, sharing features, and Audience Network advertising), and as required by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game progress data is kept while your game account exists so your progress is there when you return. You may ask us to delete your stored game data at any time by emailing sammanlamsal9@gmail.com; we will delete it and confirm by reply. Data stored locally on your device can be removed by clearing the app/browser data or uninstalling.

8. Children

Knife Slash is not directed at children under 13, and Facebook Instant Games requires players to have a Facebook account under Facebook's own terms. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Security

Game data inside Facebook is stored on Facebook's infrastructure and protected by Facebook's security measures. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we have designed the game to hold as little data as possible — only gameplay progress.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date. Continued use of the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: MASARP STUDIO, sammanlamsal9@gmail.com.
